RONIN

THE CURSED MARCH

Your lord fell at Sekigahara — and would not stay dead.
He raised the army. He raised your friends. He is still calling you.

Click once and he is yours. From then on he simply follows the cursor — no button held.
Take the pointer off the window and he obeys for five more seconds, then waits for another click.

Click — he dashes that way.   Double-click — he flashes at the nearest enemy.
Hold anywhere — he plants his feet and draws. Let go: the Iai, a cut in every direction.
His katana cuts on its own. SPACE also throws the Iai.

Do not meet a gorgon's eye — turn your back to her.
And the living are still out there. Reach them.
